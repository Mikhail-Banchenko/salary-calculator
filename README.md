Salary calculator

Simple monthly salary calculator in Belarusian rubles. Adds up daily gains, subtracts card fees and work spendings, and shows the final salary with statistics. For personal use only.

Pick a month and year, tap a day in the calendar, and enter that day's gain and card operations. The app adds them up.

Formula: `(total gain − total card operations × 0.5) × percent − spendings`.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the GitHub Pages site. Nothing is saved.
